One modular-monolith web app (working name SunPower Ops: Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL) takes a customer from first enquiry to the end of a 20-year panel warranty. The MVP is scoped to build and demo in 24 hours; every later phase is called out separately. [v1.1] The MVP now includes a customer portal (secure expiring links, no login), four team members, and the added features listed in Part 1.

6.3 Phases
Phase 0: Foundations (hours 0 to 2)
Auth.js credentials with Argon2id and database sessions; requireRole guard and the can() matrix from TRD 2.6.
Phase 1: Enquiry to qualified lead (hours 2 to 5)
Objective: no enquiry can be lost (C1) and the customer can see their request. Depends on: Phase 0.
Phase 2: Survey and AI site assessment (hours 5 to 7)
Objective: a sized, surveyed and approved site (C2, C12). Depends on: Phase 1.
Phase 3: Quote and BOM (hours 7 to 10)
Objective: a correct quote from a template in one click, which the customer can accept (C3). Depends on: Phase 2.
Acceptance: 3 kW On-Grid with a 25 m cable run yields the 12 lines in TRD 2.4; sending before approval returns SURVEY_NOT_APPROVED; a sales discount above 5% returns DISCOUNT_NEEDS_APPROVAL; a quote below target margin shows the warning; version 1 becomes read-only after a revision; the customer accepts on the portal after the last-four check.
Phase 4: Orders, payments, reservation, scheduling (hours 10 to 13)
Objective: a confirmed order reaches the warehouse and crew automatically (C4, C5). Depends on: Phase 3.
Phase 5: Inventory, QR, purchasing and dispatch (hours 13 to 16)
Objective: every serialized unit is scanned out, linked to its customer, and its warranty starts on the right event (C6, C7). Depends on: Phase 4.
Phase 6: Installation field app (hours 16 to 18)
Objective: proof of work with customer acceptance (C8). Depends on: Phase 5.
Acceptance: sign-off with fewer than 3 photos returns PHOTOS_REQUIRED; sign-off with any checklist item missing returns CHECKLIST_INCOMPLETE; sign-off sets the order INSTALLED, issues the certificate and computes profit; a draft survives a reload; the gallery shows survey photos as Before; every screen works at 360 px.
Phase 7: Service and warranty (hours 18 to 20)
Objective: support handles a breakdown call in seconds, the customer can raise and track requests, and billing is correct (C9). Depends on: Phase 5.
Phase 8: Analytics, finance, predictive maintenance (hours 20 to 22)
Objective: the director sees which jobs made money and what is unfinished (C10, C11). Depends on: Phases 4 to 7.
Phase 9: Hardening and release (hours 22 to 24)
Objective: the demo scenario passes end to end and the system is documented for another team. Depends on: all phases.
Acceptance: the demo test passes three runs in a row; no serious axe violations on those screens; every API route has an authorization test; no portal response contains a UUID or another customer's data; production is live.
6.4 Verification after each phase
Run the phase's unit, integration and end-to-end tests.
Read every failure; never mark a phase done on assumed success.
Walk the phase's acceptance criteria on staging and tick them off.
